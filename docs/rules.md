# Game rules

Move one exposed skewer to an empty slot on another accessible tray. A tray holds up to three skewers. Filling all three slots with identical food clears the group. An empty tray reveals its next reserve plate, and any complete group that appears clears immediately. Clear all food and complete any required orders before the grill timer runs out.

The timer counts foreground play and pauses when the player pauses or leaves the game. A move that starts with time remaining resolves in full. If a blocked grill has no move, the player can undo, use an applicable booster, or restart. Undo restores the latest skewer move while keeping the elapsed time. Using a booster clears the undo checkpoint. A reward posts once for the first clear of a level or challenge. Order and wooden tray bonuses post when the board is won.

## Customers and trays

- A food cover opens after a matching group of the pictured food clears.
- Ice loses a layer when a qualifying group clears. Frozen trays cannot send or receive food.
- A HOT tray has a separate deadline. Empty its visible and reserve plates before it expires.
- Clear a group on a cedar tray to earn its bonus when you win.
- An order advances when its requested groups clear. The interface shows whether the order is optional or required and how much time remains.

## Boosters

- **Collect** removes one matching group from exposed food and counts it toward eligible orders.
- **Shuffle** redistributes exposed food and empty slots across accessible trays.
- **+30 sec** extends the grill clock by 30 seconds.
- **Open** removes one selected food cover or ice block.

A successful booster uses one item from inventory or charges its displayed coin price. An ineligible action leaves the wallet and board as they were.

## Challenges

- **Master Chef** requires clearing the grill and serving the chef’s featured order.
- **Conveyor Chaos** allows pickups from the first two trays. The front tray rotates to the back after each move.
- **Stacked Orders** presents four required orders in sequence.
- **Match & Drop** slides food left after each move and exposes deep reserves as trays empty.
- **Color Match** groups foods by their color badge, so a group can contain different foods in the same family.
